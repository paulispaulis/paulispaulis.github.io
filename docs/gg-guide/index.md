## How to be a GOOD GARDENER for this world

This is not a guide about tending plants in the soil, but on living a beautiful life.

Nurturing growth, beauty and life around you. As a Good Gardener, your duty is to cultivate clarity and take purposeful action in your life, and inspire those around you to do the same.

### What is a Good Gardener?

A Good Gardener (my definition) nurtures growth in this world, first their own, then of those around them, radiating outward as far as their capabilities allow.

This role requires two main elements: clarity and action.

Clarity is about understanding yourself deeply – your thoughts, emotions, desires, fears – and using this understanding as a guiding light in your life. It involves having clear goals that align with who you truly are and what you truly want.

Action is the practical aspect of being a Good Gardener. It's not enough to just have clarity; you must also act on it. This means making conscious choices that support your goals and values every day.

In terms of the divine feminine and masculine energies that exist within us all, being a Good Gardener involves the divine masculine learning how to nurture the natural force of life that is the divine feminine, not to tame or influence, but to encourage and give room to radiate and grow. Creating an environment where the divine feminine feels acknowledged, appreciated, and free to express herself. It's about being the gardener who nurtures the seed, allows it to grow, and watches it bloom into a radiant flower.

## Good Gardener Guide overview

This guide is structured in several parts, each focusing on a different aspect of being a Good Gardener. You can jump to whichever part resonates with you right now.

### ADIYOGI:

Are you tired of living life on autopilot, carried away by distractions and the chaos of the world? Do you feel like your actions are guided more by circumstances than by your own will? Do you wish you could regain control of your mind and actions, instead of being swayed by the loudest voices and the strongest influences?

Imagine a life with clarity of thought and action, where every moment is a conscious dance of purpose and joy, an intimate embrace with life. This is the life of Adiyogi, a symbol of enlightenment and tranquillity, a beacon of purposeful living.

In this guide, we'll embark on a transformative journey, piecing together from various practices a unique path that works for you - a path that will strain and then soothe your body and mind, helping you learn, grow, and evolve. We'll explore problem questions that resonate deeply with our struggles and provide practical solutions for them. Whether you feel lost and joyless, overwhelmed by the noise of the world, or disconnected from your surroundings, we have a practice for you.

Strive for a state where you're not just surviving life, but making love with it. A state where every action is deliberate, infused with curiosity, and aligned with your unique rhythm. Let's start this journey towards self-realisation and enlightenment, towards a life that truly sparks joy.

Are you tired of feeling weak, incapable, and unattractive? Of the bare minimum physical activity that leaves you feeling mediocre at best? Do you yearn for a body that reflects strength, skill, and aesthetics, much like the gods of old? Then allow me to introduce you to a transformative journey that will elevate you from ordinary to divine.

Unveil the extraordinary potential hidden beneath the layers of neglect and disuse. Discover the balance and harmony of dance, bodybuilding, acrobatics, and martial arts, that when combined, bring forth a body that is both capable and aesthetically striking. In this guide, you will learn to tap into the power of discipline, consistency, and hard work, and mold your physical form into a living testament of human possibility.

Imagine no longer being restricted by the limited capabilities of your body. Instead, imagine being good at almost any activity on the first go, making the most of life's opportunities, and radiating an energy that invigorates every facet of life.

This isn't just about curating a body; it's about carving out your place among the gods. Begin your journey towards a divine transformation, my friend. Your ascendance starts here.

### ODYSSEY:

Are you tired of living a life dictated by society? Do you feel like you're simply going through the motions, working on tasks that lack passion and purpose? It's time to break free and start writing your own Odyssey.

In a world where everyone seems to be dictating what you should do, this guide dares you to take control. To go beyond self-improvement and to improve the world around you, too.

This guide will teach you how to identify problems that resonate with you, engage with them passionately, and craft solutions that are meaningful and impactful.

Problem-solving as a pathway to self-actualisation. Alongside practical tips on project management and time allocation, it also encourages you to harness the potential of your subconscious mind. Learn to channel your thoughts and energies towards your priority projects, even in the midst of life's everyday distractions.

So, are you ready to take the reins and embark on your own Odyssey?

### PROMETEUS:

Finally remember to teach. Don't hoard what you've discovered. If it was enough to help you, it is enough to help someone else. Now go have fun!
