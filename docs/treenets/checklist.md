## Safe‑Start Treenet Checklist

### 1) Permissions & Legality

- Verify local rules (public vs private land)
- Get written permission when needed
- Neighbour/park considerations (noise, hours)

### 2) Tree Health Screening

- Species and diameter fit for load (avoid immature/decaying trees)
- Red flags: fungus/conks, deep cracks, dead crowns, soft bark
- When in doubt, consult an arborist

### 3) Tree Protection

- Padding at contact points (both trunk and redirects)
- Prevent abrasion and bark compression

### 4) Forces & Safety Factor

- Plan for static load only; avoid dynamic loading
- Target safety factor: 4:1 minimum
- Angle awareness: shallower angles increase anchor load

### 5) Gear Essentials (rated only)

- Slings/webbing/rope with known ratings
- Connectors (carabiners/shackles) with ratings
- What to buy first vs borrow/rent

### 6) Indoor Winter Drills

- Knot practice (bowline variants, fig‑8, alpine butterfly, prusik)
- Panel weaving on a table (ground-level)
- Doorway/beam anchor simulations

### 7) Field‑Day Plan

- Site selection and span plan (sketch + roles)
- Setup sequence and inspection checks
- Redundancy at critical points

### 8) Teardown & Inspection

- Post‑use inspection (webbing wear, hardware, bark)
- Pack and log gear condition
- Leave No Trace
